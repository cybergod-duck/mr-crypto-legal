Privacy Policy

Mr. Crypto Discord Bot · BCR Research Stack · Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Mr. Crypto Discord bot and related BCR crypto services (the "Service"), how we use it, and your choices.

1. Who Operates the Service

The Service is operated by the BCR Research stack ("we", "us"). For privacy questions, contact us via Telegram: @bcr_token_bot or @bcr_channel_bot.

2. Information We Collect

DataWhy
Discord user ID, username, display name Identify your session, apply rate limits, and keep short conversation context
Messages you send to the bot (mentions, DMs, slash commands) Generate replies and maintain recent chat history for continuity
Timestamps & usage counts Rate limiting, abuse prevention, and operational logs
Server / channel IDs (when you use the bot in a server) Deliver responses in the correct channel; basic operational logging

We do not ask for your seed phrase, private keys, or government ID through the bot. Never send those in chat.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve bot responses
  • Maintain short-term conversation memory (recent turns only)
  • Enforce rate limits and prevent abuse
  • Operate premium verification when you use linked Telegram payment flows

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI Processing & Third Parties

When you message the bot, your message (and limited recent context) may be sent to third-party AI API providers to generate a response, including:

  • xAI (Grok) — primary voice model
  • Together AI — fallback if primary is unavailable

Discord hosts the chat platform and processes data under its own policy: Discord Privacy Policy.

On-chain payments (if you use premium gateways) are public blockchain transactions — we do not control public ledger visibility.

5. Retention

  • Conversation history: recent turns stored locally for context; not kept indefinitely as a full archive.
  • Logs: short operational logs may be retained for debugging and security.
  • Premium records: subscription/payment verification records kept as long as needed to provide the service.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect stored data. No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for your Discord account and wallet security.

7. Your Choices

  • Stop using the bot at any time by removing it or ceasing DMs.
  • Request deletion of stored conversation data by contacting us on Telegram with your Discord user ID.
  • Do not include sensitive personal data in prompts.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. International Users

Data may be processed in the United States or wherever our servers and API providers operate. By using the Service, you consent to that processing.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

Telegram: @bcr_token_bot · @bcr_channel_bot
